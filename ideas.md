# School Website Design Direction

## Approach 1
**Theme Name:** Editorial School Journal

**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, editorial identity inspired by printed school annuals, modern museum wayfinding, and a well-loved campus noticeboard. Layered paper tones, deep ink blue, and a precise coral accent make the school feel established, human, and quietly ambitious.

**Probability:** 0.07

## Approach 2
**Theme Name:** Garden Campus

**Very Brief Intro:** A light, optimistic visual language built from botanical greens, sunlit photography, and spacious compositions. It would emphasize belonging, calm, and a school culture rooted in curiosity and care.

**Probability:** 0.03

## Approach 3
**Theme Name:** Night Lab

**Very Brief Intro:** A dark, high-contrast direction with electric blue details and luminous overlays, positioning the school as a forward-looking hub for technology, research, and creative experimentation.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Direction: Editorial School Journal

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design with references to Swiss schoolbooks, independent magazines, and civic wayfinding systems. The interface should feel composed and tactile rather than ornamental.

### Core Principles
1. **Print logic, digital ease:** Use strong typographic hierarchy, generous margins, hairline rules, and clearly sequenced sections while keeping navigation obvious and interactive.
2. **Warm authority:** Pair ink navy with paper cream and a bright coral signal color so the school feels credible without becoming institutional or cold.
3. **Human evidence:** Use photography as documentary evidence of campus life, paired with short captions, dates, and direct language.
4. **Quiet motion:** Motion should feel like a page turn or a pinned card settling into place: short, directional, and purposeful.

### Color Philosophy
The base is paper cream rather than pure white, creating a softer reading surface and a tactile print reference. Ink navy carries trust and legibility. Vermilion coral is reserved for actions, section markers, and moments that need attention. A muted olive appears sparingly as a secondary campus note. The colors should feel discovered in an archive rather than poured from a gradient generator.

### Layout Paradigm
Use a left-hand editorial rail on wide screens, offset content blocks, and alternating wide/narrow reading measures. The home page should lead with an image-led hero, then move into a split principal letter and a staggered set of school signals. Interior pages should have a slim page index and strong page titles instead of generic centered hero blocks.

### Signature Elements
- A coral vertical rule and chapter number at the start of every major page section.
- Small uppercase metadata labels that behave like cataloguing notes: `FIELD NOTE`, `ISSUED`, `COMMUNITY`, `ADMISSIONS`.
- A recurring offset paper-card treatment with subtle grain, one hard edge, and one rounded corner.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interactive element should feel like an invitation to move through the school archive. Links underline or shift by a few pixels; buttons compress on press; cards lift minimally and reveal a coral edge. Menus should be immediate, clear, and forgiving on touch.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for hover and focus states. Stagger hero text by 60ms increments. Images enter with a shallow 12px vertical settle and opacity transition; carousels crossfade and translate 8px rather than snapping. Ambient hero shapes move only when motion is allowed, and all non-essential animation is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for page titles and feature headlines; use **Manrope** for body text, navigation, controls, and metadata. Headlines are sentence case with tight tracking and restrained line height. Metadata is 11–12px uppercase with 0.14em letter spacing. Body copy is 16–18px with a 1.65 line height and a 65ch maximum measure.

### Brand Essence
**Positioning:** A public-facing school for families who want rigorous learning, visible character, and a campus that contributes to its community—not just a timetable.  
**Personality:** Assured, observant, generous.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and quietly optimistic. CTAs use active verbs and specific outcomes. Microcopy should sound like a thoughtful school office, never like a startup funnel.

Example lines:
- “A place to practice becoming.”
- “See how the day is made.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a simple open book formed from two offset coral brackets, with a small navy star at the spine to represent the moment a question becomes knowledge. The symbol works without text as a favicon and beside the school name in the header.

### Signature Brand Color
**Vermilion Signal — `#E85D4A`**. It is warm, legible, and ownable: a visual cue for action, school spirit, and the small moments that deserve to be noticed.

### Style Decisions
- Preserve the editorial school-journal direction across all pages.
- Prefer off-white paper surfaces, ink navy type, coral signals, and thin rules over generic white cards or purple gradients.
- Keep school photography documentary and bright enough to feel welcoming; place dark text only on light image-safe areas and use overlays where necessary.
- Treat the logo as a visible brand asset in the header and as the favicon, not as a tiny decorative afterthought.
